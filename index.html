Install in 4 steps

  1. Download the ISO of your edition and check its SHA256.
  2. Write it to a USB stick (8 GB or more) with Rufus or balenaEtcher.
  3. Boot from the stick (UEFI recommended) and pick Install.
  4. Choose your language and keyboard, and follow the installer.

Recommended: 4 GB of RAM and 20 GB of disk space.

Verify your download

Compare the SHA256 checksum of your file with the one shown in the table above.

Linux / macOS

sha256sum orenOS-xfce.iso

Windows (PowerShell)

Get-FileHash .\orenOS-xfce.iso -Algorithm SHA256

FAQ

Which edition should I choose?

Xfce for a light and fast system, KDE for a full-featured desktop, Flashback for the classic GNOME 2 feeling.

Is orenOS based on Debian?

Yes, on Debian 13 “Trixie”. Debian packages and updates work as usual.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. The ISO starts a live session; choose Install when you want to keep it.

Does it work in a virtual machine?

Yes. We test with VMware: give it 4 GB of RAM and a 20 GB disk, and enable UEFI.

Is it free?

orenOS is built from free software. Each component keeps its own licence.
